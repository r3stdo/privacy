It's a Calendar (달력이야) Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-21 · Operator: KIMDOHYUN · Contact: r3stdo@gmail.com

1. Information we handle

  • Calendar and Reminders data: The app accesses your device's Calendar and Reminders to show, add and edit events and to-dos. This data is processed only on your device and is never sent to the operator's servers.
  • App settings, anniversaries and checklists: Theme, language, anniversaries and checklists attached to events are stored only on your device (and in the storage shared between the app and its widgets). A backup file is created only when you export it yourself.
  • Advertising information: In the free version, Google AdMob may collect a device identifier (IDFA, if you allow app tracking), IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. In regions such as Europe, a consent screen (UMP) is shown first.
  • Payment information: The one-time purchase to remove ads is handled by Apple. The operator does not receive your payment details.

2. How we use it

  • Showing and editing events and to-dos, widgets and notifications
  • Serving ads to free users and checking whether ads were removed by purchase

3. Sharing with third parties

  • Google AdMob (advertising). Ads are not loaded for users who purchased ad removal.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it with other third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Data inside the app is deleted when you delete the app. Your original calendar and reminder data stays in your calendar accounts (iCloud, Google, etc.) and is not owned by the app.

5. Permissions

You can turn off access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars / Reminders / Tracking.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page. Contact: r3stdo@gmail.com
